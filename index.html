📚 Training Principles

Progressive Overload

  • Increase weight gradually
  • Keep proper form
  • Log everything in Hevy
  • Aim for top of rep range

Rep Ranges

  • Compound : 6–10
  • Machines : 8–12
  • Isolation : 12–15
  • Rear Delts : 15–20

Rest Between Sets

  • Heavy Press : 2–3 min
  • Rows : 2 min
  • Isolation : 60–90 sec
  • Cardio after workout
