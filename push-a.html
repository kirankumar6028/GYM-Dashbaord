1️⃣ Incline Barbell Press

Primary Muscle Upper Chest ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Secondary Front Delts • Triceps
Movement Heavy Compound
Sets 4
Reps 6–8
Tempo 3-1-1
Rest 2–3 Minutes

🎯 Why?

  • Main upper chest strength builder.
  • Progressive overload exercise.
  • Improves pressing power.

💡 Tips

  • Bench at 30°.
  • Retract shoulder blades.
  • Touch upper chest.
  • Control the eccentric.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Bouncing the bar.
  • Using too much incline.
  • Half reps.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Incline Dumbbell Press
  • Smith Machine Incline Press

2️⃣ Flat Dumbbell Press

Primary Muscle Middle Chest ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Secondary Front Delts • Triceps
Movement Compound
Sets 3
Reps 8–10
Tempo 2-1-2
Rest 2 Minutes

🎯 Why?

  • Greater ROM than barbell.
  • Improves chest symmetry.
  • Builds overall chest thickness.

💡 Tips

  • Stretch at the bottom.
  • Don't slam dumbbells together.
  • Maintain wrist alignment.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Too much shoulder involvement.
  • Very short range of motion.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Machine Chest Press
  • Barbell Bench Press

3️⃣ High-to-Low Cable Fly

Primary Muscle Lower Chest ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Secondary Inner Chest
Movement Isolation
Sets 3
Reps 12–15
Tempo 2-1-2
Rest 60–90 Seconds

🎯 Why?

  • Maximum chest stretch.
  • Improves lower chest definition.
  • Excellent finishing movement.

💡 Tips

  • Keep elbows slightly bent.
  • Squeeze the chest at the bottom.
  • Move slowly.

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Using excessive weight.
  • Bending elbows too much.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Pec Deck Fly
  • Dumbbell Fly

4️⃣ Pec Deck Fly

Primary Muscle Inner Chest ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Focus Peak Contraction
Movement Isolation
Sets 3
Reps 12-15
Rest 60 Seconds

✅ Benefits

  • Maximum chest squeeze.
  • Constant tension throughout the movement.
  • Improves mind-muscle connection.

💡 Tips

  • Pause for 1 second while squeezing.
  • Don't let the weights crash together.
  • Control the negative.

❌ Avoid

  • Using momentum.
  • Leaning forward.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Cable Fly
  • Dumbbell Fly

5️⃣ Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Primary Front Delts ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Secondary Side Delts • Triceps
Movement Compound
Sets 3
Reps 8-10
Rest 2 Minutes

✅ Benefits

  • Builds shoulder strength.
  • Adds overall shoulder size.
  • Improves overhead pressing ability.

💡 Tips

  • Keep core tight.
  • Don't arch the lower back.
  • Lower until elbows reach 90°.

❌ Avoid

  • Locking elbows aggressively.
  • Using body momentum.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Machine Shoulder Press
  • Barbell Overhead Press

6️⃣ Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Primary Side Delts ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Movement Isolation
Sets 4
Reps 12-15
Rest 60 Seconds

✅ Benefits

  • Creates wider shoulders.
  • Improves V-taper.
  • Excellent isolation movement.

💡 Tips

  • Lead with your elbows.
  • Raise to shoulder height.
  • Use light to moderate weight.

❌ Avoid

  • Swinging dumbbells.
  • Shrugging traps.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Cable Lateral Raise
  • Machine Lateral Raise

7️⃣ Reverse Pec Deck

Primary Muscle Rear Delts ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Secondary Upper Back • Rhomboids
Movement Isolation
Sets 3
Reps 15-20
Tempo 2-1-2
Rest 60 Seconds

✅ Benefits

  • Builds rear delts for balanced shoulders.
  • Improves posture.
  • Reduces shoulder imbalances.

💡 Tips

  • Lead with elbows.
  • Pause during contraction.
  • Keep chest against the pad.

❌ Avoid

  • Using momentum.
  • Shrugging shoulders.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Face Pull
  • Bent-over Rear Delt Fly

8️⃣ Overhead Rope Extension

Primary Muscle Long Head Triceps ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Movement Isolation
Sets 3
Reps 10-12
Tempo 2-1-2
Rest 60-90 Seconds

✅ Benefits

  • Best movement for the long head.
  • Improves overall arm size.
  • Deep stretch under load.

💡 Tips

  • Keep elbows close.
  • Stretch fully.
  • Separate the rope at lockout.

❌ Avoid

  • Flaring elbows.
  • Using body momentum.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Overhead Dumbbell Extension
  • EZ Bar Skull Crusher

9️⃣ Rope Pushdown

Primary Muscle Lateral & Medial Triceps ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Movement Isolation
Sets 3
Reps 12-15
Tempo 2-1-2
Rest 60 Seconds

✅ Benefits

  • Excellent finishing exercise.
  • Constant cable tension.
  • Builds triceps definition.

💡 Tips

  • Keep elbows pinned.
  • Spread the rope at the bottom.
  • Control the eccentric.

❌ Avoid

  • Moving shoulders.
  • Using excessive weight.

🔄 Alternatives

  • Straight Bar Pushdown
  • Single Arm Cable Pushdown
  • Reverse Grip Pushdown
